# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and users of kra-secure, regardless of:
- Age, body size, or disability
- Ethnicity or national origin
- Level of experience
- Education
- Socio-economic status
- Gender identity and expression
- Marital or family status
- Mental and physical ability
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Political belief
- Race or religion
- Sex or sexual orientation

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

✅ Using welcoming and inclusive language
✅ Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
✅ Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
✅ Focusing on what is best for the community
✅ Showing empathy towards other community members
✅ Helping others learn and grow
✅ Giving credit and recognition
✅ Following the project's documentation and guidelines

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

❌ Harassment or discrimination of any kind
❌ Insulting or derogatory comments
❌ Trolling or spam
❌ Publishing private information without consent
❌ Deliberate misinformation
❌ Threats or violent language
❌ Unwelcome sexual advances or comments
❌ Exclusionary jokes or behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. 

Maintainers who do not follow the Code of Conduct may face temporary or permanent removal from the project.

### Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document it** - Save screenshots, links, or context
2. **Report privately** - Email kraaxiom@gmail.com with:
   - Description of the incident
   - Who was involved
   - When it occurred
   - Any supporting evidence
3. **Allow time for response** - Expect a reply within 5 business days

All reports are reviewed confidentially. We will not publicly identify reporters unless they request it.

### Response Process

1. Initial acknowledgment within 24 hours
2. Investigation of the incident
3. Communication of findings to all parties
4. Enforcement action if warranted
5. Follow-up to ensure resolution

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, etc.)
- Official project events
- Project communications channels
- Representing the project in public spaces
